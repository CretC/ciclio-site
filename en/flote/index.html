Ciclio for fleets.

We are building your fleet’s deadlines in one place: inspections, insurance, road vignettes, services and drivers’ documents, in a shared space with several dispatchers.

  • Car inspection
  • Car insurance
  • Road vignette
  • Services
  • Licences, certificates
  • Several dispatchers
  • A company space, where you will invite colleagues by role (admin, editor, read-only). When someone leaves, the vehicles will stay with the company.
  • Totals at a glance: you will see deadlines in the next 30 days, overdue items and yearly cost, with filters and CSV export.
  • A daily digest: on a day with many deadlines, you will get one notification listing them all instead of dozens.

What it isn’t: only deadlines and documents. No GPS, no driver tracking, no maintenance or parts management. Other tools do that; Ciclio doesn’t try to replace them.

Status: in preparation. We have no price or launch date to announce yet. Leave your address and we’ll tell you when it’s ready, no obligation.
